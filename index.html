Introduction

About me

I'm a full stack developer who enjoys taking a product from idea to production. I work across the whole stack: building the interface in React and Next.js, designing the APIs and data layer in Node.js, and deploying and maintaining it on Linux servers.

Most of my work involves the parts that make a product real: payments, identity verification, SMS and email, Shopify integrations, and background jobs and caching that keep things fast. I've built products for finance, e-commerce, real estate, and events, and I'm comfortable both starting from scratch and improving an existing codebase.

I started with a BCA degree and a MERN stack internship, then worked as a software engineer at Acoustte before joining Esferasoft Solutions, where I currently build and maintain full stack products in a team using Git-based workflows.

Outside of work I stay active with fitness, cook, and go on long drives to recharge.
